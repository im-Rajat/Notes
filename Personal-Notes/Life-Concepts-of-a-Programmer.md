# OOPS vs Life Concepts (Life story of a Programmer)


## Core Object-Oriented Principles & Access Modifiers

- Encapsulation taught us to hide our feelings.
- Inheritance taught us to carry heavy expectations,
- Polymorphism taught us to wear different faces for different people.
- Abstraction taught us to overlook the painful details.
- Single Inheritance taught us to stay loyal to one source until the end.
- Public taught us to be available,
- private taught us to hide our deepest pain.
- protected taught us to trust selectively,
- final taught us that some things can never be changed.

## Memory, Storage & Cleanup

- Memory Management taught us that holding on too long can cause problems,
- So sometimes life needs a ruthless garbage collector.
- CLR (Common Language Runtime) taught us to remove unnecessary objects for better memory management.
- Destructor taught us, at the end, cleanup matters. Don't leave pending regrets, broken promises, or unclosed connections.
- File Handling taught us to save precious memories in permanent storage, rather than keeping them in volatile runtime memory.
- And some memories are marked read-only; they never get overwritten.

## Error Handling & System Failures

- We encapsulate our feelings, inherited expectation, polymorphed our personalities, abstract our pain, yet life still throws unhandled NullPointerExceptions.
- Still, life threw exceptions that no try-catch could handle.
- Exception Handling taught us to face the crisis in the try, accept failure in the catch, and close the connections finally.

## Data Structures & Execution Logic

- Array taught us be with same mindset people.
- Lambdas taught us to focus strictly on our core function, cutting out the noise and drama.
- Algorithms taught us to optimize everything, trying to do the absolute best for everyone.

## Version Control & State Management

- Branches taught us how easy it is for paths to completely diverge.
- Merges taught us that combining two different worlds always results in conflicts.
- Commits taught us that the mistakes you make are permanently written into history.
- Stashes taught us how to hide my current chaos just to look functional for a moment.
- Life taught us that no matter how many times I reset, I can never undo the damage.
- `git reset --hard HEAD` History erased, but the scars remain

## The Ultimate Code Reviewer (Life, AI & Heartbreak)

- Life taught us that none of it was enough.
- Life is the ultimate code reviewer; SOLID principles make better software, but life's principle make a better engineer.
- "Oops!" is carrying a lot of responsibility here.
- In the end, life treated us like temporary objects.
- But at the end AI taught us that nothing is of any use.

## Dependency & Disconnection

- She was my API. I made requests. She responded. Then the endpoint changed and my calls just failed.
- She kept me as a separate Branch. Never Merged me into her Main. Said I was still in Review.
- She wasn't just a row in my datbase. She was the entire schema. When she left, every query in my life started returning NULL
